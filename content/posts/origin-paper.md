In this paper we show that our new mass-spectrometry system detects organic molecules at very low abundances — down to femtomoles per mm<sup>2</sup>, a billionth of a millionth (10<sup>-15</sup>) of a mole. Still a great many molecules.

### [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-66240-1) / [PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-66240-1.pdf)

### TL;DR

In this paper we present:

- ORIGIN — a compact, lightweight laser desorption ionization mass spectrometer designed for the detection of extremely low concentrations of amino acids on in-situ space exploration missions.
- A detection limit of a few fmol mm<sup>-2</sup>, with a path to sub-fmol sensitivity — exceeding current space exploration systems by three orders of magnitude.
- Evidence that ORIGIN is a strong candidate instrument for life-detection missions such as the Europa Lander.
