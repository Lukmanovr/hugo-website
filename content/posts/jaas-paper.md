### [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1039/D0JA00390E) / [PDF](http://wurz.space.unibe.ch/Tulej_JAAS2020.pdf)

### TL;DR

In this paper we present:

- Chemical depth profiles of a highly heterogeneous medium — an amygdale of calcium carbonate embedded in the surrounding basalt.
- Oxides and sulphides within the inclusion material provide the basis for elemental-abundance calibration factors.
- With RSC corrections, quantitative analysis of the more complex mineralogical phases inside the inclusion, by correlating atomic abundance fractions in ternary diagrams.
- Stoichiometric abundances reconstructed with the proposed method.
