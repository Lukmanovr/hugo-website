## Short bio for organizers

Rustam Lukmanov is an Associate Professor and Deputy Director of the AI Institute at Innopolis University, working on machine learning for graph-structured data and efficient deep learning. He holds a Ph.D. in experimental physics from the University of Bern, where he developed unsupervised methods for laser mass spectrometry instruments designed for space exploration.
